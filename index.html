01 — The Bloom

The Bloom

A single rose, grown for one purpose.

It is cultivated in one row, on one slope, and cut in the hour its colour peaks — never before, never after. Nothing about it is left to weather or chance. It was never meant to stay a rose.

02 — The Release

The Release

Petals fall and dissolve into liquid pigment.

The bloom lets go the way a held breath does. What falls is not lost — it thickens, darkens, and settles into the exact colour of the moment just before dusk. Eleven days, undisturbed.

03 — The Form

The Form

Pigment sets into a precise matte bullet.

Poured warm, cooled slowly, drawn from the mould in one unbroken piece. The bevel is cut to an edge fine enough to draw the line you actually meant. Weight you notice in the hand.

04 — The Finish

The Finish

One continuous motion from pickup to application.

It meets the mouth without announcement — the way silk meets skin. Colour that stays where you left it through dinner, and softens, kindly, as the evening goes on.

The Film

She does not wear the colour. The colour learns her.

01 — Craftsmanship

The Fall

Harvested before the light turns. Separated by hand, petal by petal, because a blade bruises colour and a hand does not.

02 — Craftsmanship

The Infusion

Pressed cold and left to steep until the pigment holds its own weight. We stop the moment it stops deepening. Not a day past.

03 — Craftsmanship

The Setting

Poured at forty degrees into a chilled brass mould, then left in silence for six hours. Cooled too quickly, it clouds. So it is never hurried.

04 — Craftsmanship

The Hand

Every bullet is turned, weighed and finished by one pair of hands from mould to case. If it does not stand true on the bench, it does not leave it.

05 — Craftsmanship

Nine hundred roses become one shade.

A single case holds an entire season of one slope. We make what the harvest allows, and when it is finished, it is finished until the roses come back.
